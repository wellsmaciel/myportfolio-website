# Portfolio

This context describes the professional portfolio and the audience it serves.

## Language

**Primary audience**:
Recruiters and hiring managers assessing candidates for junior or graduate Full Stack development roles.

**Target market**:
Roles based in Ireland or the United Kingdom, plus remote roles at employers based anywhere.

**Remote role**:
A position the candidate can perform remotely from Ireland, regardless of the employer's headquarters.

**Developer-first profile**:
The portfolio presents Full Stack development as the candidate's primary direction and uses prior IT support experience as a complementary strength.

**Functional job descriptor**:
A plain-language description of the work performed; it may be clearer to portfolio readers than the employer's formal job title.

**Project evidence**:
Deployed projects, supported by concise explanations of the candidate's contribution, technical choices, and verified results, are the portfolio's primary evidence of development ability; courses and certifications support that evidence.

**Project prominence**:
The Projects section follows About so a recruiter encounters development evidence early.

**Project source link**:
A public code repository may be linked alongside a project's deployed app when the candidate can share that source.

**Portfolio-ready repository**:
A public project repository with a README that orients visitors and documents verified features, technologies, and setup steps.

**Current section**:
The portfolio section corresponding to the content a visitor is reading; its navigation link remains visually highlighted.

**Navigation accent**:
The current section, hover, and keyboard-focus states use the same theme-specific accent color as H2 headings.

**Sticky navigation**:
The compact portfolio menu remains available during page scrolling, while the active item tracks the current section.

**Demonstrated technical skills**:
Technical competencies listed on the portfolio should be supported by the candidate's projects or other concrete experience shown there.

**Career narrative**:
Lead with the Full Stack direction, frame senior IT support as complementary experience, and retain finance as brief background context.

**Language profile**:
The candidate is bilingual in Portuguese and English, with intermediate Spanish.
